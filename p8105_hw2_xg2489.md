The orginal dataset “NYC_Transit_Subway_Entrance_And_Exit_Data.csv” has
32 variables related to each entrance and exit for each subway station
in NYC. The raw data has 1868 rows and 32 columns. Column names is
standardized through “janitor::clean_names”, key variables are kept and
“entry” is converted from a character variable into a logical variable
using “case_match”. After cleaning the data , This “subway_df” dataset
contains 19 variables including line, station name, station latitude &
longitude, routes served (from route 1 to route 11), entry, vending,
entrance type, and ADA compliance. The size of dataset is 1868 rows and
19 columns. This data is not tidy because the routes served by a station
are spread across 11 separate columns with too many null values and thsy
should be stored as a single variable.

During the import process, the dataset is imported using skip = 1
because the first row contains variable descriptions. Missing values
which are encoded as “.” in the orginal dataset are transformed to na
argument so that R treats them as NA. Records with an onset age earlier
than the baseline age are removed as they do not meet the stated
inclusion criteria (i.e. no MCI at baseline). The variables “sex” and
“apoe4” are transformed from binary numeric codes into factors
(“Female”/“Male” and “Non-carrier”/“Carrier”) because they are
categorical variables. A total of 483 participants were recruited. After
excluding 3 participants who did not meet the inclusion criterion, the
cleaned dataset contains 480 participants. The variables are `id`,
`current_age`, `sex`, `education`, `apoe4` and `age_at_onset`.

Of the 483 recruited participants, 3 were excluded because they have
already developed MCI at baseline. Among the remaining 480 participants
who met the inclusion criterion, 94 developed MCI during follow-up. The
average baseline age is 65.03 and the proportion of women in the study
who are APOE4 carriers is 0.3.
